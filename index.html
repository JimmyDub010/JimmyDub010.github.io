Making more games accessible

Welcome! I'm JimmyDub, an independent developer working on accessibility mods and software. My goal is to make more games and tools usable by blind and visually impaired players, including people who rely on screen readers and keyboard controls.

This site brings my projects, downloads, progress updates, and support links together in one place.
