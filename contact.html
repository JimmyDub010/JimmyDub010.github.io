Contact and feedback

Feedback is useful whether a project works perfectly or has a problem. Please share what you tried, what happened, and the game or software version you used.

Before publishing

Add the public contact method you want people to use here, such as a dedicated email address, a Discord invite, or a relevant project issue page. Avoid posting personal information you do not want public.

Helpful bug-report details

  • Project name and version.
  • Game or software version.
  • Steps you took.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • If it worked, what you tested successfully.
