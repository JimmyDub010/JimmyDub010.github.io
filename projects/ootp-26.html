Out of the Park Baseball 26 Accessibility Mod

Accessibility work for Out of the Park Baseball 26, a baseball management game.

Current status: In progress; some screens and workflows remain difficult.

Work so far

  • Play-by-play information can be narrated for screen-reader use.
  • Some in-game management actions, including pinch hitters and pitching changes, have been made more accessible.

Known limitations

Some screens still require work. Injury-list workflows have been a particular obstacle.

Availability

This project is in progress. A download link will be added when a release is ready.

Report a problem or share a success

Feedback from people who test the project helps guide future development. Please include the game version and what happened.
