Support my work

I build accessibility mods and software so blind and visually impaired players can access more games and tools. Support helps cover the AI coding tools and other development resources that make this work possible.

Transparency

I plan to post regular development updates and monthly screenshots or other progress evidence so supporters can see what the work is going toward. Any screenshots shared publicly will be checked for private account information first.

Support helps fund the work, but it does not guarantee a particular feature or release date. Projects vary in complexity and may run into technical limits.

Requesting a project

In the future, I may offer a higher support tier for people who want to suggest or request projects. Requests would be considered based on feasibility, accessibility impact, and available time; payment would not guarantee that a particular mod can be completed.
